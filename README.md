# 🟠 Main Features (100% optional and configurable!)
 
* **Add hand-crafted structures to your Minecraft world!**
* **Add unique randomly generated [modular dungeons](https://nightbreak.io/plugin/betterstructures/#dungeoneering-modules-free) to your world!**
* **Use pre-made packs or build your own with WorldEdit!**
* **Fits any world generator.**
* **Structures spawn on the surface, underground, in the sky and on liquid surfaces.** [Placement types](https://wiki.nightbreak.io/BetterStructures/creating_generators#structuretype)
* **Chests with randomized loot.**
* **Optional vanilla mob spawns inside structures.**
* **Contains custom props and furniture when used with the [BetterStructures Prop Pack](https://nightbreak.io/plugin/freeminecraftmodels/#betterstructures-prop-pack)**
* **Only generates in new chunks.**
* **Supports custom items.**
* **[EliteMobs](https://nightbreak.io/plugin/elitemobs/) integration** for advanced **boss fights**.
* **MMOItems** & **MythicMobs** integrations.
* **Download, install and update content packs from an in-game menu** with `/bs setup`. [Content packages](https://wiki.nightbreak.io/BetterStructures/content_packages)
* **Pregenerate your world with `/bs pregenerate`**, which pauses when the server's TPS drops. [Commands](https://wiki.nightbreak.io/BetterStructures/commands#utility-and-admin-commands)
* **Choose which worlds get structures** in `ValidWorlds.yml`. [How](https://wiki.nightbreak.io/BetterStructures/faq#how-do-i-disable-structures-in-specific-worlds)
* **Apply plugin updates without restarting** with `/bs applypluginupdate`. [How it works](https://wiki.nightbreak.io/BetterStructures/commands#about-bs-applypluginupdate)
* **Fast & lightweight** with **no noticeable server impact** when exploring.
* **Want an even better experience?** Works great with [EliteMobs](https://nightbreak.io/plugin/elitemobs/), [Free Minecraft Models](https://nightbreak.io/plugin/freeminecraftmodels/), [Resurrection Chest](https://nightbreak.io/plugin/resurrectionchest/), and [Resource Pack Manager](https://nightbreak.io/plugin/resourcepackmanager/) — all free!
 
> **Important:** Make your own content or **download at least one** of the **free packs** below so structures can start spawning.
 
# 🟠 Requirements & Recommendations
 
* **Required:** [WorldEdit](https://dev.bukkit.org/projects/worldedit) or [FastAsyncWorldEdit](https://modrinth.com/plugin/fastasyncworldedit)
* **Recommended:**
 
  * [**EliteMobs**](https://nightbreak.io/plugin/elitemobs/) for boss fights
  * [**WorldGuard**](https://dev.bukkit.org/projects/worldguard) for region protection
  * *Note:* EliteMobs-specific packs (**Shrines 1 & 2**, **Caves & Lost Civilizations** Free & Premium) require **EliteMobs**.
 
> ⚠️ **Do not use** [AsyncWorldEdit](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/asyncworldedit.327/) — it **will break** things.

# 🟠 Performance hints
 
<details>
<summary><strong>Open performance tips</strong></summary>
 
**For normal servers**
 
1. **Check your server view distance.** This is in `server.properties`. Higher view distances generate exponentially more chunks — a view distance of 40 can result in \~5000 chunks loaded **per player**.
 
**For low‑end servers**
 
1. **Make sure you have enough RAM.** Rough guideline: **\~3 GB total** available for the server (Minecraft + plugins), not that BetterStructures itself uses that much.
 
2. **Alternatively, pregenerate your world.** Use `/bs pregenerate` or a world pregenerator such as Chunky with BetterStructures installed to bake structures into the map. After generation, BetterStructures uses essentially no power. Downside: you can’t add new structure packs to that world without generating a new one (same as vanilla).
 
</details>
